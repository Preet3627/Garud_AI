# Garud AI v0.1.3 — Voice Stability & Wake Word Precision

This release focuses on improving the reliability of the Hands-Free Voice Mode and fixing precision issues with custom wake word detection.

## 🎙️ Voice Mode Enhancements

### ⚡ Improved Response Timing
- Optimized audio interruption logic to ensure the AI stops speaking immediately when a new wake word is detected.
- Fixed a bug where Hands-Free mode would fail to resume if the AI had an empty response or only punctuation to say.
- Smoother transitions between "Listening" and "Speaking" states.

### 🎯 Precision Wake Word Detection
- **Regex Sanitization**: Custom wake words are now properly escaped. You can now use special characters in your wake word without breaking the AI logic.
- **Improved Text Cleanup**: Enhanced the algorithm that removes the wake word from your spoken command, preventing "I am listening" or "Hey Garud" from being sent to the AI as part of the question.
- **Case Sensitivity Fix**: You can now type your wake word in any case in the settings; the engine handles the normalization automatically for better recognition.

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Internal Improvements
- **Version Sync**: Improved version normalization across the app components.
- **File System**: Fixed incorrect path references for release notes documentation.
- **Voice Feedback**: Added "I am listening" voice prompt when the assistant is manually triggered via wake word to provide better user feedback.
